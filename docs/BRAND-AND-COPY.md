# Brand and Copy Direction

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-21
**Scope:** Brand identity, visible property copy, HTML title/description and related project documentation.

## Identity

- **Working concept brand:** Kaven & Rowe
- **Featured property:** The Glass Pavilion
- **Positioning line:** Private Residences · Architecture & Place
- **Voice:** restrained, assured, architectural, precise and cinematic
- **Primary language:** English

Kaven & Rowe is a working fictional/concept identity for this demonstration property page, not a claim that a real estate business operates from the displayed page. The footer retains the original concept/build attribution.

## Naming research

A small public-web screening was run on 2026-10-21 for names associated with luxury residential property:

- Search results surfaced the existing [Auren Properties](https://aurenproperties.com/en/) real-estate/development site and a [2026 AUREN Estates concept-brand post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/graphicauthor_branding-brandstyleguide-brandguideline-activity-7502764618403168256-AFfD). To avoid an unnecessary overlap, Auren Estates was not used.
- Exact-name searches for **Kaven & Rowe** did not surface a clear current real-estate agency/property-development brand match in the sampled results. The returned results were not evidence of an established same-name property brand.

This was an initial public-web screen only. Search results are incomplete and can change; the name has **not** been legally cleared, checked against all company registries or checked through a full trademark search. Do not treat this note as legal advice or proof of exclusivity.

## Copy principles

1. Lead with what is distinctive about this residence—light, glazing, material, landscape and spatial sequence—rather than piling on generic luxury adjectives.
2. Prefer concise, concrete language that works beside photography.
3. Vary sentence openings and avoid repeating the same phrase across the hero, room panels and lifestyle section.
4. Preserve the existing section order, headings' approximate lengths and explicit line breaks to minimise any incidental text-flow changes.
5. Treat measurements, room areas, bedroom/bathroom counts, internal area, grounds and guide price as fixed source facts. Do not invent new facts or change values while editing prose.
6. Keep the viewing-form message honest: the form is a demonstration and neither sends data nor makes an appointment.

## Metadata approach

The title names both the property and concept brand without repeating keywords. The meta description summarises the actual page and notes that the viewing form is demonstration-only.

This follows [Google Search Central's title-link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) (descriptive, concise title text) and its [snippet/meta-description guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet) (relevant, accurate page summary rather than keyword strings).

## Visual and source boundaries

The rebrand must not change media files or paths, asset-manifest contents, CSS/layout, animation code, recovered baseline files, the 302-frame hero sequence, or the existing photo set. Only brand/copy, metadata, relevant package/console labels, tests of the updated content contract, and documentation are in scope.
